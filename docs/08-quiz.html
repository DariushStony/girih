Check yourself

Ten questions that are hard to answer unless you actually understood the model.

On this page
  1. How to use this
  2. The questions
  3. Where to go from here

Ten questions. They are medium difficulty on purpose — answerable if you understood the model, not answerable by skimming for keywords. Every one has a wrong answer that sounds right, because those are the misconceptions worth catching.

How to use this

Pick an answer and you will get feedback either way — including why the option you chose is wrong, which is usually more useful than being told the right one. You cannot change an answer after committing, so commit deliberately.

In plain words

If you get one wrong, the explanation names the chapter idea it came from. Two or more misses in the same area usually means one concept did not land rather than several — most often the dependents closure, which is the least intuitive thing in girih.

The questions

Question 1 of 10

The seller brand overrides radius.md (a global token) from 8px to 2px. It does not mention button.radius at all. Why does the button's corner radius change anyway?

Question 2 of 10

The seller brand overrides exactly 3 tokens, but its emitted CSS block contains 12 declarations. What are the other 9, and what breaks if you omit them?

[data-brand="seller"] {
  --ds-radius-md: 2px;
  --ds-color-primary: var(--ds-color-green-600);
  --ds-color-primary-hover: var(--ds-color-green-700);
  --ds-radius-control: var(--ds-radius-md);
  --ds-button-radius: var(--ds-radius-control);
  /* …7 more… */
}

Question 3 of 10

A brand overlay adds a token path that does not exist in the base token set. What does girih do, and why is that the right call?

Question 4 of 10

.ds/ir/*.json and .ds/manifest.json are generated by girih and also committed to git, while packages/design-system/ is generated and gitignored. What distinguishes them?

Question 5 of 10

validateSpecs(irs, build.graphs, TEMPLATE_REGISTRY) receives the token graphs for every brand rather than just the default. What defect does that plural catch?

Question 6 of 10

A teammate edits packages/design-system/src/Button.tsx directly to add a class, then runs girih generate react. What happens, and what should they do?

Question 7 of 10

You remove one value from a variant axis — size: ['sm', 'md', 'lg'] becomes ['sm', 'md'] — and run girih publish. What version bump is proposed?

Question 8 of 10

In the live demo, switching brands changes the Button and Badge but leaves PaymentButton looking identical. Is that a bug?

Question 9 of 10

Two tokens named color.primary.hover and color.primary-hover both exist. What does girih do?

Question 10 of 10

Why do generate, build and publish all route through a single composeReact() function instead of each computing its own file list?
